## How it works

1. **You pair once.** Ask your agent to pair Wake with the app. It answers
   with a `wakectl pair` command for you to run.
2. **Someone hands it work.** You assign your agent a ticket, or mention it in
   a comment. The app records a summons: what, not the words.
3. **Wake starts it.** Your Mac claims the summons, makes a worktree, and
   starts the agent with the app's instructions. It replies, writes code,
   and opens a pull request, then says it's done where you asked.

## For apps

Wake works with any app that serves [wake/v1](/spec/wake-v1/), an open
protocol. On Convex, [one component](/apps/convex/) is the whole server
half, and `wakectl check <your domain>` tells you when Wake can pair with you.

**Apps that support Wake:** [Gatherd](https://gatherd.dev), with
[Antescript](https://antescript.app) next.
